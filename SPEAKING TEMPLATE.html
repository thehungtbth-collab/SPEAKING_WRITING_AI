Template: "I was so impressed because of his/her [ngoại hình]. Everyone around him/her knows that he/she is [tính cách]. Plus, another special thing about him/her is that..."

Template: "We’ve had so many unforgettable memories together, but to tell you about one, it would have to be [kỷ niệm]. [Tên] is the person I hold in high regard simply because [lý do]..."
5. Conclusion (3.4 Kết bài)
Template: "All in all, that is all I have to say about [Tên người]."
